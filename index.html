About Coral Connect

A meeting place for everyone who cares about the reef

Coral Connect is an annual event organised by Indonesian Blue Foundation that brings together marine scientists, local communities, students, entrepreneurs and government. We share knowledge, build partnerships and turn ideas into real coral restoration along the coast of Lombok.

This fourth edition focuses on connecting local knowledge with new technology, and on making reef restoration a shared responsibility. [FINAL TEXT FROM HENRY]

Why reefs matter

Small animals, huge impact

25%

of all marine life depends on coral reefs, on less than 1% of the ocean floor.

97%

of wave energy can be absorbed by a healthy reef, protecting the coast.

Coral Triangle

Indonesia lies in the most biodiverse reef region on earth.

+1 °C

above normal for a few weeks can already cause coral bleaching.

1–2 cm

is how much many corals grow per year, which makes restoration patient work.

Millions

of people in Indonesia rely on reefs for food and income.
